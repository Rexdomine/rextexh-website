## 3. Product / stakeholder direction
- RexTexh was founded in 2019 as an IT solutions company delivering websites, software, server management, networking, and related technology services on a contract basis.
- The business performed well technically but relied heavily on referrals and lacked a repeatable marketing system for attracting high-value clients.
- Rex shifted focus to other ventures and the company became inactive.
- Rex now wants to revive RexTexh with completely different positioning: not as a generic software agency, but as the business ecosystem behind his public AI education and consulting work.
- Rex's Prompt-to-Code with Rex content is the top-of-funnel audience engine. The website should bridge content discovery into consulting, implementation, and paid engagement.
- The company should be positioned around modern AI-powered services, including:
  - AI software development
  - AI automation and workflow design
  - Custom web and mobile applications
  - Internal AI assistants and agent systems
  - Technical consulting
  - AI implementation for businesses
  - Private coaching and mentoring
  - Corporate AI training
- The educational side should become part of the RexTexh ecosystem through a structured learning hub on the website, where tutorials can be browsed by skill level, topic, or learning path rather than remaining isolated on YouTube.
- The website should make it easy to book meetings directly via Calendly for one-time projects, long-term consulting, private mentoring, corporate training, and technical advisory work.
- The commercial tone should be credibility-first rather than aggressively sales-driven. The site should quietly demonstrate proof, capability, and clarity so visitors naturally want to hire Rex or learn from him.
- The long-term goal is an AI-first technology brand that combines education, consulting, software development, and AI implementation into one cohesive ecosystem.
