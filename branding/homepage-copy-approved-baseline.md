## 3. How RexTexh Works

### Eyebrow
How RexTexh works

### Headline
From unclear AI idea to working business system.

### Intro copy
AI becomes valuable when it is connected to a real business problem. RexTexh helps you understand the workflow, choose the right solution, build it properly, and improve it after people start using it.

### Method blocks

#### 01 — Understand the operation
We look at how the work currently happens: what your team repeats, where information gets stuck, what slows people down, and what could be made simpler.

#### 02 — Decide what should be built
Not every problem needs a chatbot. Some need a dashboard, an automated follow-up, a better form, a custom app, a staff assistant, or simply a clearer workflow.

#### 03 — Build the practical system
RexTexh develops the software, automations, internal tools, or AI assistants needed to support the workflow in real use.

#### 04 — Help your team use it
A useful system is not finished just because it launches. We help make the workflow clear enough for people to understand, adopt, and improve.

## Approval notes

This revision keeps the premium RexTexh tone but makes the homepage clearer for an average business visitor who knows about AI generally but does not know how it can be applied inside a business.

Main changes from v1:

- Removed the Founder / Brand Bridge section from the homepage.
- Reworked the hero to focus on saving time, improving workflows, and building better systems.
- Added a “You might need RexTexh if...” section to explain real business pain points.
- Added concrete examples under each capability.
- Made the Workflow Console example more everyday and less abstract.
- Made the final Book a Call section speak directly to visitors who know AI could help but do not know what to build first.
