## Quick evaluation
### What works
- The wordmark reads as **RexTexh** more clearly than earlier attempts
- The crown is more premium and more controlled than the earlier renders
- The logo is visibly less crest-like and less esports-driven than the original identity system
- The navy / restrained gold behavior supports the authority + premium direction
- The horizontal lockup is commercially credible enough for first review

### Remaining weaknesses
- Some viewers may still mentally autocorrect the unfamiliar name to **RexTech**
- The crown still carries a bit of sharp emblem energy, so the “calm” attribute is only partially solved
- The concept is still exploratory, not yet typographically or optically finalized
- Monochrome and small-size proofing have not yet been fully established in a deterministic vector workflow
