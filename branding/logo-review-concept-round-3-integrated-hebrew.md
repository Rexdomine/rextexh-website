## Quick evaluation
### What works
- English reads clearly as **RexTexh**
- Hebrew reads clearly as **מלכי צדק**
- The Hebrew now feels integrated into the design rather than appended below it
- The crown-led direction remains calm, premium, and more business-credible than the original crest identity
- The lockup has a stronger sense of unity and visual intention than the earlier subtitle-based version

### Remaining weaknesses
- The mark still has some generated-image character and would need deterministic redraw for final polish
- At very small sizes, the Hebrew line may require a separate simplified variant or tighter optical handling
- The crown and oversized `T` still compete slightly for hierarchy and may need refinement in a final vector stage
