## 2. System / Method Section

### Eyebrow
How RexTexh works

### Headline
From AI idea to working business system.

### Intro copy
AI becomes valuable when it is connected to the way a business actually works. RexTexh helps you move from vague interest in AI to clear workflows, useful tools, and practical implementation.

### Method blocks

#### 01 — Understand the operation
We start by looking at the real workflow: where time is lost, where decisions slow down, and where better systems could create leverage.

#### 02 — Design the right solution
Not every problem needs a chatbot or a complicated AI stack. We define the simplest useful system before anything is built.

#### 03 — Build the workflow
RexTexh develops practical software, automations, internal tools, and AI assistants that fit the way your business needs to operate.

#### 04 — Improve with use
After launch, the goal is not just to have something running. The goal is to make the system clearer, faster, and more useful over time.
