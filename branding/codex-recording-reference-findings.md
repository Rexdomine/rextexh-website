## Verified visual direction
- Centered hero: actual app identity, short Codex title, one-line explanation, one compact CTA. Clear hierarchy over an expansive atmospheric background; not a two-column wall of headline text.
- Strong blue/periwinkle/lavender cloud-like color fields. The correct lesson is NOT to eliminate gradients; it is to concentrate them into a deliberate visual scene while keeping foreground content simple.
- Code-like ASCII clusters are subtle foreground/background accents, not large floating marketing labels. Full-resolution marks include circle-like characters, angle marks and dashes; they are not unambiguously only binary 0/1.
- First four seconds show changing clusters near cursor locations, with older clusters persisting briefly and fading after movement away. This supports a trail/persistence interpretation, not a rigid pointer-following object. Exact idle timeout and emission mechanism cannot be proven from sampled footage.
- Early trust logos followed by a large recognizable product UI. Product visuals carry meaning rather than serving as decorative panels claiming a fictional product exists.
- Bright atmospheric hero transitions into dark editorial/product sections. Header remains visible and changes contrast. Do not claim exact scroll-trigger implementation from video.
- Feature sections alternate substantial product imagery with concise explanatory blocks. Their imagery demonstrates skills, parallel work, scheduled tasks and review findings.
- Later layout shifts to three usage surfaces, a testimonial grid, then a spacious atmospheric final CTA. Cards are appropriate here because they organize actual choices or evidence; cards themselves are not the problem.
- Consistent visual treatment ties the hero, product illustrations and final conversion together without making every section identical.

## Corrections to earlier RexTexh interpretation
1. Clean does not mean colorless or static: this reference is vivid and atmospheric.
2. Premium does not mean every element gets a glow, pill container or shadow.
3. Large type works with short text and designed line lengths, not a full sentence forced into a narrow column.
4. The design alternates calm identity, concrete demonstrations, evidence and action. It does not repeat the same promise in many grids.
5. Keep RexTexh's real crown/wordmark and business positioning. Do not copy Codex branding, client logos, product claims or testimonials.
6. Translate the visual demonstration into an understandable business input → useful output, clearly labeled if illustrative. Do not revive a fictional RexTexh OS console.
7. The approved long-form copy remains a source, not an excuse for density. Any shortened homepage copy must be presented for approval.
