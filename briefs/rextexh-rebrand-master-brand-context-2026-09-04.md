## 1. Executive summary
RexTexh is being revived from a former 2019 IT solutions company into an **AI-first technology brand and ecosystem**.

The brand is no longer meant to be framed as a generic IT vendor or traditional software agency.

Its intended role is to sit at the intersection of:
- AI implementation for businesses
- software and automation delivery
- internal assistant / agent systems
- technical consulting and advisory
- education-driven trust building
- private mentoring and corporate AI training

The commercial positioning should remain **credibility-first, practical, modern, and quietly confident** rather than hype-heavy or aggressively sales-driven.

## 10. Logo strategy and approved direction
### Strategic logo conclusion
The old identity had useful equity in:
- the Rex = authority concept
- crown symbolism
- blue-led color memory
- confident presence

But it was visually trapped in:
- mascot logic
- crest architecture
- esports/gaming energy
- heavy illustration and banner styling

### Chosen modernization direction
**Crown-Led Abstract Technology Mark**

Why it won:
- preserves crown equity
- carries authority clearly
- feels more credible for an AI-first company
- scales better across web, documents, and future product surfaces
- removes the outdated mascot / crest category

### Approved logo direction now locked
The approved concept direction is:
- horizontal lockup
- crown on the left
- `RexTexh` wordmark on the right
- no Hebrew
- no subtitle
- dark blue geometric crown
- central white diamond negative space
- small gold diamond above the crown center
- double-base crown structure
- dark blue custom-feeling wordmark with prominent `R` and tall `T`

### Non-drift rule
Future work must preserve:
- crown-left / wordmark-right structure
- central white diamond negative space
- small gold diamond accent
- double-base crown structure
- exact `RexTexh` spelling
- no-Hebrew decision

Future work must avoid:
- reintroducing Hebrew
- converting the logo into a stacked or unrelated architecture
- replacing the crown with another symbol family
- drifting into a new concept without explicit approval
