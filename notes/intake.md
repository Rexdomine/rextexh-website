# Intake Notes — RexTexh Rebrand

Status: Vision captured. Awaiting deeper strategy breakdown and execution authorization.

## Founder background
- RexTexh was founded in 2019 as an IT solutions company.
- Original services included websites, software, server management, networking, and related contract-based technology support.
- Technical delivery was strong, but growth depended mostly on referrals.
- The company did not build a consistent system for attracting high-value clients.
- Rex later focused on other ventures and RexTexh became inactive.

## Current rebrand direction
- Rex wants to revive RexTexh with new positioning.
- Instead of operating as a generic software agency, RexTexh should become the business brand behind Rex's AI content, consulting, implementation work, and education ecosystem.
- Prompt-to-Code with Rex is a key upstream audience channel and should feed discovery into RexTexh.

## Website role
- The website should act as a funnel from content to consulting.
- Visitors arriving from YouTube or social media should quickly understand that Rex both teaches AI and builds real systems for businesses.
- The site should not feel aggressively sales-driven; it should build quiet credibility.
- Desired outcome: visitors naturally decide they want Rex to build for them, advise them, or teach them personally.

## Intended service ecosystem
- AI software development
- AI automation and workflow design
- Custom web and mobile applications
- Internal AI assistants and agent systems
- Technical consulting
- AI implementation for businesses
- Private coaching and mentoring
- Corporate AI training

## Education ecosystem intent
- Tutorials should become part of a structured on-site learning hub.
- Content should eventually be organized by skill level, topic, and learning path.
- The learning hub should transform disconnected videos into a cohesive educational asset.

## Conversion intent
- People should be able to book Rex directly through Calendly.
- Booking paths may include:
  - one-time projects
  - long-term consulting
  - private mentoring
  - corporate training
  - technical advice

## Strategic ambition
- RexTexh should become an AI-first technology brand.
- The long-term system should combine education, consulting, software development, and AI implementation into one cohesive ecosystem.
- As the audience grows, the website should become a self-sustaining lead generation engine powered by content, open-source work, client proof, and thought leadership.
